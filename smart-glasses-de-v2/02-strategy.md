# Go-To-Market & Product Strategy: KlarBlick AI (`smart-glasses-de`)

## 1. Core Positioning Statement
> *"For German engineers and professionals who need hands-free AI assistance without legal or workplace friction, **KlarBlick AI** delivers lightweight, German-engineered smart glasses with physical privacy shutters, on-device EU-hosted AI, and pre-certified Betriebsrat compliance—turning wearable computing into a trusted everyday tool rather than a surveillance risk."*

## 2. Target Audience Segments
1. **Primary (B2B Mittelstand & Industrial):** Field-service technicians, quality assurance engineers, and maintenance leads in German manufacturing, automotive, and medical technology.
2. **Secondary (B2B/Prosumer Knowledge Workers):** Architects, consultants, and executives needing real-time translation, teleprompter notes, and hands-free navigation without bulky headsets.

## 3. Three Strategic Pillars
1. **Hardware-Enforced Privacy (DSGVO & Sec. 201 StGB Safe):**
   - Physical sliding camera shutter that mechanically blocks the lens.
   - Dedicated hardware microphone kill-switch that severs power to the mic array.
   - High-visibility amber status LED hardwired to sensor power.
2. **Betriebsrat-Ready Enterprise Deployment:**
   - Configurable **Zero-Telemetry Mode** (disables all cloud sync, camera, and audio logging for restricted factory zones).
   - Turnkey *Muster-Betriebsvereinbarung* (model works agreement) and DPIA documentation included in every pilot kit.
3. **All-Day Prescription Comfort:**
   - 38 g titanium frame compatible with standard German optician (*Optiker*) prescription lenses.
   - Monochrome green micro-LED waveguide HUD for crisp text, checklists, and live translation without obstructing eye contact.

## 4. Pricing & Channel Model
- **Enterprise Pilot Kit (5 Units + MDM & Legal Pack):** EUR 4,490
- **Standard Device MSRP:** EUR 799 (Hardware) + EUR 19/user/month (EU Sovereign AI & MDM Cloud)
- **Go-To-Market Channels:** Direct B2B sales to Industry 4.0 integrators + B2B2C prescription fulfillment via partner opticians across DACH.
